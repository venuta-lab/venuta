# 09: What people who keep their own music actually ask for

Asked 2026-10-13: research other platforms and user forums for what people do and want, and how
Venuta could do it better; a numbered list to pick from.

Sources, read 2026-10-13: Roon's community ("Top 10 Feature Requests", Radio threads); Plexamp
and Symfonium documentation and reviews; the MusicBee Wishlist; Spotify's community (shuffle,
Smart Shuffle, local files); Hacker News threads "Apple Music is the last library focused music
service" (238 comments), "Is there a cloud music library that doesn't keep deleting my music?"
(123) and "Petrichor, an offline music player" (106); Wavelet and AutoEq; Apple's iOS 26 lyric
pronunciation; the Dynamic Range Database and collectors' write-ups. Reddit and Head-Fi could not
be read from here (blocked), so their voices come only second-hand.

Effort: S (a day or less), M (a few days), L (a week or more). "Online" means a free public
service, used only when you ask; "account" means you sign in.

## Listening
1. **Library radio, local only** (M). Start a radio from any song, album or playlist that plays
   only your files, endlessly, and says why each song came ("shares 3 playlists with Kesariya",
   "same composer, 2008"). Roon and Plexamp users love radio for rediscovering their own
   collection, and complain it drifts to streaming tracks. Venuta has nothing else to drift to.
2. **Guest DJ modes** (S, after 1). Plexamp-style: "more like this", "a short detour", "keep the
   mood". Each is a rule on the same signals as 1, shown in the queue.
3. **Fair shuffle** (S). Spotify's most-voted complaint: shuffle clumps songs by album and artist
   and keeps replaying the same 30 songs. Shuffle that spreads artists and albums apart, plus a
   "rediscover" shuffle that favours songs you haven't heard in a while.
4. **Fade on pause and stop** (S). A Roon request; also a short fade when you skip.
5. **Wake to music** (S). An alarm beside the sleep timer, also a Roon request: a playlist at a
   time, rising slowly in volume.
6. **Per-song rules** (S). Never in shuffle, skip the spoken intro, start at 0:12, always play
   with the next song (live albums). HN library keepers asked for "rules to skip songs".

## Your library
7. **Credits and filters, Roon-style** (M). Browse and filter by composer, lyricist, singer,
   label, year, format and quality, combined, with the filters inverted or saved. This is Roon's
   most praised power feature (Focus), and Venuta's lookups already filled in composers and
   lyricists.
8. **Versions of an album** (M). Two copies of one album, or a remaster and the original: shown
   as versions of one album with a "primary" pick; the rest hidden from lists, but kept. Roon's
   box sets and versions are among its top requests.
9. **Dynamic range per album** (S). The loudness-war number collectors check (DR, as on the DR
   Database), measured by Venuta itself in the deep check, beside the album and on its versions,
   so you can keep the better master. It fits the fake hi-res and lossy checks Venuta already
   makes.
10. **Library check-up for bit rot** (M). Every file's audio checksum is already known: re-check
    them on a schedule and report a file that changed or can't be read, with the backup copy to
    restore from. "Backups, bit-rot protection, integrity checks" came up six times in one HN
    thread.
11. **Backup to a drive** (M). Mirror the whole library to an external or network drive the way
    cards are written (only changes, never deleting what Venuta didn't put there), with a
    reminder when it is out of date.
12. **Browse by folder** (S). Asked of Roon and MusicBee Remote: for people who think in
    folders.
13. **Search across scripts** (S). "kesariya" finds केसरिया, and the other way round (rule-based
    transliteration, as in 21). HN library keepers named Hindi and Japanese metadata as a pain.
14. **A spreadsheet view of songs** (M). Columns you choose, sorted and filtered as you type
    (Petrichor's thread asked twice), with editing in place.
15. **Albums by colour** (S). Browse the wall by cover colour, from the palette Venuta now
    computes (asked in Petrichor's thread; nice for "I remember it was the blue one").

## Playlists
16. **Smart playlists, grown up** (M). Symfonium's best-loved feature. Nested rule groups (this
    AND (that OR that)), "played in the last month", "not played since", "added this week",
    "rated 4 or more"; a size limit picked at random each time you open it, or by most played.
17. **Covers for songs you don't have** (M, online). From the plan: each missing album's cover
    looked up once and kept.
18. **Playlist changes in Activity, with Undo** (S). From the plan.
19. **Playlists and waiting songs in Music's search** (S). From the plan.
20. **Date added as a diary** (S). "Added this month", "a year ago today", a "memory journal" of
    what came into the library when (HN). Also feeds 25.

## Lyrics
21. **Lyrics in Latin script** (M). Devanagari (Hindi, Marathi) and other scripts shown in Roman
    letters, by rule, offline. Apple added this for Hindi in iOS 26, only for some songs and only
    for subscribers; Venuta can do it for every song with lyrics.
22. **Make synced lyrics** (M). Tap along once for songs that have only plain lyrics; Venuta
    writes the timed .lrc (an undoable change).
23. **Lyrics on the big screen** (S). A full-window Now Playing with large synced lyrics for the
    TV or a second screen (Roon asked for a screensaver and TV display).

## History and you
24. **Bring over Apple Music's history** (M). Play counts, loved songs, ratings and dates added
    from the library file, so stats are right from day one. Carrying play counts and date added
    over came up three times in Petrichor's thread. (Format research first.)
25. **Your year in music** (M). Hours, top songs, albums, composers and singers, the film year
    you played most, streaks, the album you came back to. Stats came up repeatedly; Wrapped-style
    tools only see streaming, never your files.
26. **ListenBrainz** (S, account). Send what you play, at half the song or 4 minutes, by its
    own rule. It is open and free; Last.fm too if you want.

## Sound
27. **Headphone correction** (M, online once). AutoEq's thousands of corrections, or one imported
    from a file (Reference Audio Analyzer has the Mega5EST), as parametric EQ with a preamp so it
    never clips. Each output device remembers its own, switching on its own, as Wavelet does.
    With it off, Venuta stays bit-perfect.
28. **A parametric EQ** (M). Bands you place yourself, beside the 10-band one, as Poweramp offers
    (the engine for 27).
29. **Play to the TV or speakers** (L). Cast to DLNA/UPnP renderers on the home network. AirPlay
    and casting were asked for twice.

## Windows and phone
30. **Native Windows touches** (S). Play, previous and next on the taskbar preview; recent
    playlists and albums when you right-click the taskbar icon; optional "Listening to" in
    Discord.
31. **Your phone as a remote** (M). Roon's most repeated request: play, skip, volume, queue and
    search from a page on your home network (scan a QR code; nothing leaves the house).
32. **Send to phone** (L). Copy a playlist, or the whole library at a lower size, to an Android
    phone over Wi-Fi or USB, the way cards are written now (Roon users asked for offline sync).

## New music (online, only when you ask)
33. **New from artists you have** (M, online). A weekly "new releases" from MusicBrainz for the
    composers and singers in your library (HN asked for new-release and concert alerts), each
    with "add to To get".
34. **About this album** (S, online). A short summary (the film, the year, the composer's story)
    from Wikipedia, kept with the album; Roon users asked for album reviews.

## Speed (from the plan)
35. **The library stays in memory** (M): no full re-read on every playlist action.
36. **Apple Music's library read once**, and **Playlists does work only when something changed**
    (S).
37. **Faster start**: rarely used places load when opened (S).
38. **Measured on your real library**, before and after (S).

## Accounts (from the plan)
39. **TIDAL and YouTube Music sign-in** (M each, account).
40. **Your own server** (L): Navidrome / OpenSubsonic.

## Not on the list, and why
- Lyrics translation and "sounds like" (Plexamp Sonic Sage) need AI or a paid service: against
  Venuta's rules.
- Downloading music: never.
