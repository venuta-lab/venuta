# What must keep working

Walked through at the end of every workstream of [PLAN-REVIEW-FIXES](../plans/PLAN-REVIEW-FIXES.md)
and before every release. Each line is something a person does; ✓ when checked in the test copy.

## Music
- Home: the top card (playing, paused, picked for today) with Resume/Play and Shuffle; Jump back in; Your playlists; shelves by category, with See all
- Albums: filters (All, Hi-Res, categories), open an album, Play, Shuffle, Edit details
- Album page: songs, Play, Shuffle, play one, right-click a song, Go to artist, back
- Artists: list, an artist page with albums and songs
- Songs: the whole library, sorting by column, quality badges, double-click/Enter to play
- Playlists: list, Find a playlist, New playlist, Import playlists, a playlist page
- Search (Music): songs, albums, artists, in the lyrics, across scripts, "/" to focus
- Right-click and "…" on songs, albums, artists, playlists: Play, Shuffle, Play next, Add to queue, Add to playlist…, Go to artist, Go to album, Edit details
- Ratings on songs (Now Playing, menus); Edit details for an album

## Playing
- Play, pause (Space), next/previous (Ctrl+→/←), seek on the waveform and with keys, volume, mute
- Shuffle; repeat off/all/one; stop after this song; sleep timer
- The queue: open (Q), jump to a song, reorder by drag and Alt+↑/↓, remove, Save as playlist
- Now Playing: open/close, Lyrics (styles, Latin, timing tools), Up next, Sound (EQ, presets, tone, levelling, crossfade), Signal (bit-perfect path)
- Mini player (Ctrl+M): controls, Up next, lyrics line, pin, back to the full window
- Big screen and desktop lyrics: open, follow the song, lock/unlock, close
- Lyrics maker: tap along, save, undo from Activity
- Media keys, the Windows media panel, the tray, close-to-tray, start with Windows
- Gapless; exclusive (bit-perfect) output; the output device choice

## Playlists
- Create, rename, duplicate, delete (Set aside), export as a file
- Add songs (page picker, menus, from the queue), remove, reorder, Undo on remove
- Smart playlists: rules, live results
- Imported playlists: songs to get kept in place, Find this song, Update from the source, Complete your Apple Music playlists
- Import & export (Playlists, and a playlist's Export): Bring in and Send out
- Bring in: one box for a link, songs copied from Spotify's app, or a list of songs (Ctrl+V anywhere); files, zips and folders dropped or chosen, read by what's inside (M3U/M3U8, PLS, XSPF, JSPF, WPL, ASX, CSV, Excel, data downloads, backups); share links from Spotify, Apple Music, YouTube Music, TIDAL, JioSaavn, Deezer (and a profile's favourites), ListenBrainz; Spotify sign-in; the Apple Music library; Google Takeout; playlists from a phone find the same files here
- Where are your playlists?: every way per service with its steps; the Copy for Venuta bookmark (drag or copy, then click on a music website)
- Review (close matches, Not it remembered), Update/Keep both/Replace, Done with Undo and To get; Check them for new songs
- Send out: a file in any format with paths for this PC, next to the playlist, a phone or player, or a server; songs you don't have kept where the format can; a backup of everything (brought back, empty playlists too); YouTube Music links of 50; Spotify links to paste; Apple Music and Deezer pages of links; a CSV for TuneMyMusic or Soundiiz; copy as text
