## Design and interaction ideas (not bugs)

- **One song, one look, wherever it plays from**: the same row, card and bar for local and online
  songs; only online gets a small badge; one designed artwork fallback (a soft gradient from the
  title, not a letter); "–" for unknown length; one word for it everywhere ("Online").
- **Song changes as a crossfade**, never a blank: the bar and Now Playing keep the last cover and
  title until the next one is ready, then cross-fade; Now Playing's tools stay put, with a quiet
  placeholder while lyrics load.
- **A motion budget**: the drifting background and glass redraw only while the window is visible,
  and settle when nothing has changed for a while; playing doesn't need the backdrop moving.
- **Right-click is always Venuta's**: a menu wherever there's something to act on (artists, the
  player bar's song, the queue's songs, Now Playing's song), text fields keep Cut/Copy/Paste, and
  everywhere else nothing (never the browser's menu).
- **Drag and drop as people expect from music apps**: drag songs, albums or a selection onto a
  playlist in a drop list (or the Playlists tab), into the queue, or onto the player to play next;
  drop targets light up while dragging; a visible handle to reorder playlist songs; long lists
  scroll while you drag near the edge.
- **Big lists stay light**: the Albums grid, playlist pages and the queue draw only what's on
  screen, like the Songs list already does.
- **Home follows what's playing**, online included.
- **Empty playlists** as a compact row rather than full-size tiles.

## Works well

Local playback starts in about 126 ms and online in about 98 ms; seek, shuffle, repeat (off, all,
one), Space, next/previous, Escape order, double-click to play, and queue drag all work; mixed
local and online queues play in order and survive a restart; hybrid playlists count songs clearly
("4 songs: 3 in your library, 1 online, 13 min") and save valid M3U8; removing a song has Undo;
delete asks first and says where the file goes; right-click menus on songs, albums and playlists
are complete; searching across scripts works ("केसरिया" and "kesariya"); special characters and
emoji in search are handled; no console errors in the whole run.

## Couldn't be tested with the test catalog

Its one song is 0.2 s of silence, so these need a real online source: seeking, pausing and
resuming inside an online song; whether next plays or skips a very short online song; a song that
fails to load; going offline in the middle of an online song; an online entry that should play
your local copy instead (local first).
